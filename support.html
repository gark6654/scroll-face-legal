Email taron.alaverdyan.97@gmail.com. Include your phone model, OS version and the site you were using if something is broken.

Nods aren't recognised, or fire too easily

Run calibration again (Settings → Recalibrate) with your phone where you normally hold it and your face evenly lit. Then adjust sensitivity and cooldown in the Gestures tab.

A site doesn't scroll

Sites change their layout often. Try switching the site between Feed and Reading mode when you edit it. If it still doesn't scroll, email us the site's address.

"Continue with Google" or "Continue with Facebook" doesn't work

Those buttons don't work in in-app browsers. Set a password for that site in its own app first, then sign in inside Scroll Face with your email, phone or username and that password.

How do I pause scrolling?

Close both eyes for about half a second (a long blink) to pause, and again to resume.

I bought Pro and it isn't active

Settings → Restore purchases, and make sure you're signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us.

How do I cancel Pro?

In your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Is the camera recording me?

No. Frames are analysed on your phone to measure head angles and then discarded; nothing is saved or uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
